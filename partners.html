FAQ

Common questions from partners

How do I refer a client or book for them? +

Message us on WhatsApp (+971 58 556 5311) or introduce your client via a group chat. We handle scheduling, access instructions, and payment directly—zero admin for your team.

Will snagging delay my client's handover or transfer? +

No. We deliver the full interactive report on the same day as the inspection, giving you and your client clear documentation immediately so timelines stay on track.

Not at all. Once building access or NOC details are shared, we handle the entire on-site inspection independently and keep you updated once the report is live.
