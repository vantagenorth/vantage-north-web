Tools we use

We test and measure, not just look

Professional equipment finds the defects you can’t see with the naked eye.

  • Thermal imaging camera Hidden leaks, missing insulation and overheating wiring
  • Moisture meter Damp behind tiles, walls and wet areas
  • Water pressure gauge Pressure and flow at taps and showers
  • Socket & RCD tester Polarity, earthing and RCD trip safety
  • Laser level & measure Level floors, straight walls, true frames
  • Tile sounding tool Hollow and loose tiles before they crack
