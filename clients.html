FAQ

Common questions

Does DEWA (electricity & water) need to be on? +

Yes—having electricity and water connected allows us to load-test AC cooling, thermal performance, socket polarity, RCD trips, water pressure, and drainage falls properly. If DEWA isn't on yet, we can still inspect, though live MEP tests will be limited.

How long does an inspection take and when do I get the report? +

Inspections take 2 hours for Studios and 1-bedrooms, 3 hours for 2–3 bedroom apartments, and 4 hours for townhouses and villas. Your interactive digital report is delivered within 24 hours.

Do I need to attend the inspection in person? +

Not at all. Many of our clients are overseas or busy—just provide key collection or NOC access instructions when booking, and we handle the full inspection and digital reporting remotely.

What if the developer changes my handover date? +

Your confirmation email includes instant self-service Reschedule and Cancel buttons so you can move your inspection slot effortlessly if your developer shifts your handover.

Can you inspect after I have already taken the keys or moved in? +

While inspecting before signing the handover form gives you maximum leverage, most Dubai developers provide a 1-year Defect Liability Period (DLP) covering finishes and systems, plus a 10-year structural warranty. A formal DLP inspection documents every defect in writing so you can enforce warranty repairs before the 12-month window closes.

What exactly do you check during an inspection? +

Every accessible surface, fixture, and system—including air conditioning cooling performance and airflow, concealed moisture and waterproofing behind wet areas, electrical distribution boards and socket polarity, plumbing pressure and drainage, hollow or chipped tiles, joinery alignment, glazing seals, and paint/plaster finishes.

Do you inspect secondary-market (resale) properties as well as off-plan handovers? +

Yes. For resale apartments and villas, our inspection uncovers hidden maintenance issues, ageing AC or plumbing faults, and unpermitted modifications before you finalize the transfer—giving you objective evidence to renegotiate price or require repairs prior to closing.

What happens if the developer fails to fix everything before the re-inspection? +

Our Turn-Key package includes active developer follow-up and a comprehensive final verification re-inspection to confirm all logged defects are resolved to standard. Any remaining open items are flagged in your live cloud report with updated photo evidence so you have formal documentation to withhold handover sign-off or escalate through your DLP warranty.
