Inside The Inspection

One methodical checklist, six core systems

01

Electrical & MEP

DB labeling, socket polarity & RCD trip tests, lighting, switches, and intercoms.

02

AC & Ventilation

Cooling output, thermostat accuracy, airflow balance, and condensation drains.

03

Plumbing & Wet Areas

Water pressure, floor-waste falls, waterproofing, silicone seals, and leak checks.

04

Doors & Glazing

Frame alignment, locks, sliding tracks, acoustic/weather seals, and glass quality.

05

Tiling & Finishes

Hollow tile detection, grouting, paintwork, cabinetry alignment, and appliances.

06

Balconies & Exterior

Balustrades, external drainage, paving levels, plus villa roofs and garages.
